# Final IECON submission checklist

Use `main_final_submission.tex` and the supplied figure PDFs to rebuild the
manuscript. No stale manuscript PDF is included: compile this exact source,
then validate and upload the resulting PDF.

Before uploading, confirm:

- The PDF is exactly six pages and all fonts are embedded.
- The title, author name, affiliation, email, ORCID, and corresponding-author
  information are correct.
- The conference submission system's required copyright notice and any final
  author-registration items are complete.
- The final PDF is processed through the conference's required IEEE PDF
  validation workflow.
- The DOI repository has been updated to the exact code/results package that
  includes `run_hot_standby.py` and `hot_standby_extreme.csv` before the paper
  claims those files are released.
- The reviewer-response file is uploaded only if the submission portal asks
  for it.
- The reproducibility repository is released only after removing any private
  logs, credentials, or unpublished material and choosing a license.

Do not submit the earlier eight-page source or the rejected layout experiment.
